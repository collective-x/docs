---
title: "Collective X API"
description: "Grounded, cited answers from a peer-reviewed women's-health research corpus"
---

The Collective X Partner API gives your product access to a curated, peer-reviewed
women's-health research corpus. Every answer includes inline citations to real
papers; the API refuses to answer when the corpus lacks strong evidence.

Head to the **API Reference** tab for the two endpoints:

- **Ask a question** — `POST /v1/query`, one-shot JSON answer
- **Have a conversation** — `POST /v1/chat`, streaming or sync
